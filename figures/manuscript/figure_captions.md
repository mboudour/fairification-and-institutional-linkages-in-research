# FAIRification figure captions

## Figure 1. Study scope and Dimensions-recorded relation layers
The frozen source cohort comprises 163 publications, of which 144 exact DOI-matched publications form the final Dimensions anchor. Grant and policy outcomes are Dimensions-recorded identifier links. The diagram is descriptive and does not represent funding intent, policy endorsement, uptake, or impact.

## Figure 2. Supplementary K=3 theme linkage rates
Hard-assignment K=3 NMF component groupings and descriptive recorded-linkage rates are shown with Wilson 95% intervals. These hard-assignment summaries are supplementary only; primary association analyses use continuous NMF shares. K=3 stability is component-specific: the small heritage/preservation specialization carries a bootstrap caveat and has no K=3 leave-one-out result. Sparse policy-link counts do not support multivariable policy regression.

## Figure 3. Primary grant-link associations
Odds ratios are from the predeclared publication-year-adjusted model of a recorded grant link. NMF shares are scaled per ten percentage points and the technical metadata/semantic-workflow theme is the compositional reference. The estimates are conditional associations, not causal effects.

## Figure 4. Record-year context and policy event adequacy
Record-year bars include only recorded relation edges with both stored years; missing years are not imputed. Stored record-year differences are descriptive database-date quantities rather than causal lags. The figure also shows why policy-link models are withheld: the primary through-2022 policy window has three observed events against a predeclared 30-event requirement.

## Figure 5. Year-stratified cross-relation configurations
Observed P–G/P–D configurations are compared with the primary null that permutes P–D publication endpoint mappings within publication-year strata while holding P–G ties fixed. The result concerns structural alignment of recorded relations only; it does not identify a grant-to-policy pathway.
