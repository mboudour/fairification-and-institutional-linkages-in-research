# Reviewed manuscript figures

`figures/manuscript/` contains seven reviewed figures in PNG and PDF formats. Figures 1–5 are generated from the certified aggregate-only results bundle. Figures 6–7 are reviewed role-separated country and organization landscapes generated from the local institution-country restoration after manifest verification. Narrative captions are supplied by manuscript TeX and are not embedded in the figure files.

Figures 6–7 are a narrow reviewed exception to the otherwise aggregate-only figure release: they show selected stored country and organization labels for publication-affiliation and grant-funder panels. The selection is made from each all-anchor relation view before the frozen $K=3$ theme percentages are plotted. The sparse policy-issuer view is not plotted because only four anchor publications have a recorded issuer path.

All figures concern Dimensions-recorded identifier links, descriptive stored attributes, descriptive stored record years, or a year-stratified configuration test. They do not demonstrate funding intent, causal funding effects, institutional influence, research capacity, country effects, policy uptake, endorsement, or impact.

No raw source, Dimensions response, publication-level, grant-level, policy-document-level, identifier-level, node-level, or edge-level graphics are released. The full named tables used to produce Figures 6–7 remain private; see the [institution-country results release note](../docs/release/INSTITUTION_COUNTRY_RESULTS_RELEASE.md).
